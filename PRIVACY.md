# Privacy policy — QR code scanner

Effective date: 29 October 2026

QR code scanner identifies QR codes visible in the current browser tab and lets
you choose a decoded web link to open.

## Information processed on your device

When you enable scanning for a tab, the extension captures the tab's visible
area. These screenshots can contain website content visible on your screen.
The extension processes the pixels and QR payloads temporarily in memory to
find QR codes and display web-link previews. Screenshots and decoded results
are not written to persistent extension storage or uploaded to a server.

The extension does not use the camera, create an account, keep a scan history,
add analytics, or send screenshots or decoded results to the developer. It does
not sell user data or use it for advertising, profiling, or credit decisions.

## Opening a decoded link

A web address is opened only when you click its marker. The destination website
receives an ordinary browser visit, subject to your browser settings and that
website's privacy policy. The browser may handle cookies and browsing history
as it normally does when you open a web page.

## Permissions and control

The extension requests only activeTab and scripting. Access is temporary and
begins when you activate the toolbar button for the current tab. Click the
button again or press Escape to stop scanning; reloading or leaving the page
ends that scanning session. There are no persistent website-access grants.

Information accessed through browser APIs is used only for the disclosed
QR-scanning feature, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy,
including its Limited Use requirements. No human reviews your scanned content.
